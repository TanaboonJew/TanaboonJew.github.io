About

Software Developer at Western Digital · Thailand.

I'm Max — a software developer in Thailand, currently contributing to command-line and desktop software at Western Digital across Linux and Windows. Day to day that's mostly Rust, Python, and shell scripting. The parts I care most about are the unglamorous ones that make software hold up: automated tests that actually run, CI that fails loudly, packaging that works on both platforms, and documentation someone can follow later.

When something breaks — intermittently, or only on one platform — I want to know why before anything changes. Reproduce it, trace it to a root cause, fix it, and leave regression coverage behind so it stays fixed. Small reviewable changes over heroic rewrites; recorded verification over "trust me, it works."

Separately, on my own time: I use AI heavily in my workflow, but not in the "one prompt, ship it" way. What interests me is agents that can plan work, maintain context across a long task, and review each other's output — pipelines with checkpoints where a human still gets the final word. That fascination turned into Foreman, which has its own write-up under Work. My employer doesn't use any of it; this is a home lab obsession, not a work initiative.

Outside of work: gaming, keeping PCs running past their warranty, running local models on hardware that complains about it, reading, and writing things down so future me stops re-solving the same problems.

I build software. It works. That's generally the goal.
